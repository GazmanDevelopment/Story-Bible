1. Who is responsible for your data

Story Bible is run by Gareth Huscroft, who is the controller of your personal data under the UK GDPR. Contact: gazman.development@gmail.com.

2. What we collect and why

  • Your account: your name, email address and Microsoft account identifier, taken from your Microsoft sign-in. We use them to know who you are and which series are yours.
  • Your content: the series, characters, places, relationships, timeline events, research entries and chapter links you create. We store them so the app can show them back to you.
  • Sharing: which people you share a series with, and what they may do with it.
  • Technical logs: the time, address and result of requests to the server (see below). We use them to keep the service working and secure.

We do not use your data for marketing, we do not sell it, and there is no analytics or advertising tracking in the app.

3. Where it is kept and who can see it

Your data is kept in a database on a server run by the administrator (Gareth Huscroft). Who can see it:

  • The administrator. The admin dashboard shows account names, email addresses and counts (for example how many series and entries each account has). The administrator can also reach the database itself to run and repair the service. Story content is not read except where needed to fix a fault you have asked about.
  • People you share a series with. They can see that series and the names of the people who added entries to it.

Nobody else can see your series.

4. Other services involved

  • Microsoft handles sign-in. Microsoft tells us your name, email and account identifier. Microsoft has its own privacy policy for what it does with your sign-in.
  • GitHub is used for feedback. Issues and suggestions you send from the app are posted as public GitHub issues that anyone can read. Do not put story content or personal details in them.

We do not share your data with anyone else.

5. What is stored in your browser

The sign-in library keeps its token cache in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. It is needed for the app to work and is not used for tracking. Signing out clears it. We set no tracking or advertising cookies.

6. Logs and IP addresses

The server and the proxy in front of it record request logs, which include IP addresses. IP addresses are personal data, so we treat them as such. Logs do not contain your story content or your sign-in tokens. Logs are kept for no more than 30 days.

7. How long we keep things

  • While your account is active: your account and content are kept until you delete them.
  • Deleting your account removes your account and every series you own straight away (see the help guide). Your name on other people's series is replaced by “Deleted user”.
  • Backups: the server is backed up nightly and backups are kept for 14 days. Deleted data can remain in backups until they age out. If a backup is ever restored, earlier deletions are applied again straight afterwards.
  • Inactive accounts: an account that has not been used for 24 months is deleted. If we have a usable email address we write to you at 18, 20 and 23 months to let you know. Accounts with no usable email address are still deleted at 24 months.
  • GitHub issues are public and are not removed when you delete your account. Contact us if you want one removed.

8. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected (rectification);
  • have your data deleted (erasure);
  • receive your data in a portable format (portability);
  • ask us to restrict how we use it;
  • object to our use of it.

Most of this you can do yourself: the Account page has Download my data and Delete my account. For anything else, email gazman.development@gmail.com and we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

9. Minimum age

You must be at least 16 to use Story Bible.

10. If something goes wrong

If a personal data breach puts your rights at risk we will report it to the ICO within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and tell affected users without undue delay where the risk to them is high.

11. Changes to this policy

The version and date are at the top of this page. If we make a material change we will announce it in the app and update the version before it takes effect.
